## Status Definitions

- `VERIFIED`: Direct primary evidence supports the claim as written.
- `QUALIFIED`: Evidence supports a narrower, cautious, or contextualized version distinguishing source statements, conditional building/public-health settings, manufacturer-dependent specs, or editorial planning estimates.
- `UNVERIFIED`: Adequate direct primary evidence was not retrieved in the audit passes.
- `REMOVED`: The claim was removed from public content.
